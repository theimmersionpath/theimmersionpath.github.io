📚 Know Your Strands — Deployment Guide

Every senior high school strand has unique duties and expectations during immersion. Check your track below to see what tasks you will encounter in the field:

📖 HUMSS — Humanities and Social Sciences

School Focus: Communication, social dynamics, community engagement, and public service.

Immersion Deployment Tasks: Local government units (LGUs), barangay halls, municipal offices, day-care centers, or administrative offices. Assisting in public service documentation, organizing community outreach programs, handling front-desk inquiries, assisting teachers, and drafting reports.

🔬 STEM — Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics

School Focus: Scientific research, technical problem-solving, mathematics, and engineering concepts.

Immersion Deployment Tasks: Engineering firms, tech laboratories, hospitals, engineering offices, or research institutions. Assisting in laboratory procedures, data collection, basic technical documentation, equipment maintenance, and observing professional research practices.

📊 ABM — Accountancy, Business, and Management

School Focus: Business operations, financial literacy, management principles, and entrepreneurship.

Immersion Deployment Tasks: Business offices, accounting firms, retail establishments, or financial institutions. Basic bookkeeping, filing financial records, assisting in inventory management, customer service, and office administration tasks.

🔧 TVL — Technical-Vocational-Livelihood

School Focus: Hands-on skills development, technical proficiency, and industry-specific competencies.

Immersion Deployment Tasks: Industry-specific workplaces based on specialization — ICT (computer shops, tech support), Home Economics (hotels, food service, tourism), Industrial Arts (construction, electrical, automotive shops). Direct application of technical skills, equipment operation, quality control, and workplace safety practices.

✅ Standards and Compliance

Official requirements, expectations, and criteria for Work Immersion

⏳ Required Training Hours

Total Hours: Minimum of 80 hours of work-based learning for Grade 12 students, as mandated by DepEd Order No. 30, s. 2017.

Completion Timeline: Must be completed within the school's prescribed schedule and before graduation requirements are met.

🤝 Work Ethics — Expected Standards

Students are expected to demonstrate the following core workplace values throughout the immersion period:

  • Professionalism — Maintain appropriate conduct, appearance, and attitude at all times
  • Integrity — Be honest, reliable, and accountable for your actions and work output
  • Respect — Treat supervisors, colleagues, and clients with courtesy and dignity
  • Cooperation — Work effectively as part of a team and follow workplace instructions
  • Commitment — Complete assigned tasks diligently and within given deadlines

🦺 Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) — Usage Standards

Where applicable, students must strictly comply with PPE requirements:

  • Wear required safety gear whenever present in designated work areas
  • Inspect PPE before use and report damaged or missing equipment immediately
  • Return borrowed PPE properly after use; do not remove it from the premises without permission
  • Understand what PPE is needed for each specific task — ask your supervisor if unsure

⚠️ Safety and Health Protocols — Compliance Standards

Safety is everyone's responsibility. Students are expected to:

  • Follow all posted safety signs, warnings, and instructions without exception
  • Know the location of emergency exits, first aid kits, fire extinguishers, and emergency contact numbers
  • Report all accidents, injuries, or unsafe conditions to the supervisor immediately — no matter how minor
  • Participate in safety orientations and briefings provided by the host institution
  • Know and follow proper emergency response procedures during drills or actual incidents

📝 Documentation Requirements

  • Daily Time Record (DTR) — verified and signed by workplace supervisor weekly
  • Weekly/Monthly Activity Journal or Reflection Paper
  • Employer Evaluation Form — completed by supervisor at the end of immersion
  • Completion Certificate — issued by partner institution upon finishing required hours

📊 Grading and Assessment Criteria

Your final grade will be computed based on the official DepEd grading components:

  • 20% — Written Works: Activity journals, reflection papers, quizzes, and other written requirements
  • 80% — Performance Tasks: Supervisor's performance evaluation, completion of required hours, submission of documents, portfolio, and demonstration of workplace ethics and safety compliance

📑 Pre-Deployment Requirements

Before starting your immersion, you must submit the following to your school adviser:

  • Parent/Guardian Consent Form
  • Medical Certificate or Health Clearance
  • Insurance and Waiver Forms (as required by school)
  • Placement Acceptance Letter from host institution

📋 Attendance and Leave Policies

  • Absences must be communicated to both school adviser and workplace supervisor immediately
  • Excused absences require a medical certificate or parent letter — missed hours must be completed as make-up time
  • Excessive unexcused absences may result in extension of immersion period or failure to meet requirements
  • Change of venue is not allowed without written approval from school administration

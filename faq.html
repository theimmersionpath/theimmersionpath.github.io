❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Work Immersion

Q: What is Work Immersion?

A: Work Immersion is a required subject for Grade 12 Senior High School students. It involves 80 hours of hands-on experience or work simulation that allows students to apply their skills and knowledge in a real workplace setting.

Q: What should I do if I get sick during my immersion hours?

A: Notify your work supervisor immediately, secure a medical certificate, and inform your school adviser. Do not report to the venue if you are unfit to work.

Q: Can I change my immersion venue halfway through?

A: No. Changes to deployment venues are generally not allowed unless approved by the school administration due to valid, extreme circumstances.

Q: How are my daily tasks recorded?

A: You are required to log your daily activities and hours in your Daily Time Record (DTR) and reflection journal, which will be checked by your adviser.

Q: What should I do if I cannot report for work?

A: Inform your immediate supervisor and school focal person as early as possible. Follow your workplace protocol for filing leave or reporting absences.

Q: What do I need to prepare before my first day?

A: Bring your completed DTR form, school ID, proper uniform, pen and notebook, and a positive attitude. Review your workplace's rules and dress code beforehand.

Q: Can I use my phone while on duty?

A: Generally, no — unless it is required for your specific task. Keep your phone on silent mode and use it only during your break time or in case of emergency.

Q: What if I make a mistake at work?

A: Be honest — admit it, apologize politely, and ask your supervisor how you can fix it. Everyone makes mistakes when learning; what matters most is that you take responsibility and learn from it.

Q: Do I get paid during work immersion?

A: No. Work Immersion is part of your school curriculum and is not considered employment. However, some partner institutions may provide a small allowance — this is entirely voluntary and not required by DepEd.

Q: What happens if I don't complete my required hours?

A: You will need to complete make-up hours to meet the 80-hour requirement before you can be issued a completion certificate. Failure to complete required hours may affect your grade and graduation clearance.

Q: Is work immersion graded?

A: Yes. Your final grade is based on your supervisor's evaluation, attendance and hours completed, submitted documents, and final portfolio output.

Q: What should I wear on my first day?

A: Always wear your complete school uniform and ID unless your host institution provides a different dress code. Dress neatly and professionally — avoid casual clothes like shorts, slippers, or ripped jeans.

Q: Who do I approach if I have problems at my immersion site?

A: Talk to your workplace supervisor first if it's a work-related concern. If the issue persists or involves a sensitive matter, inform your school adviser or work immersion focal person immediately — they are there to support you.

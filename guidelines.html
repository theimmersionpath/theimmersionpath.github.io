📋 Work Immersion Ethics and Workplace Guidelines

Building Professionalism, Discipline, and Responsibility in the Workplace

Work immersion provides students with an opportunity to experience an actual working environment and apply the knowledge and skills they have learned in school. To maintain professionalism and establish a positive relationship with employers and coworkers, students must follow proper workplace ethics and guidelines.

The following guidelines serve as a reference for Grade 12 Work Immersion students to help them become responsible, respectful, disciplined, and safety-conscious individuals in the workplace.

⏰ 1. Punctuality and Time Management

Guideline: Students must demonstrate proper time management by arriving on time, following their assigned schedules, and completing tasks within the given deadlines.

  • Arrive at the workplace at least 10–15 minutes before the scheduled starting time.
  • Follow the assigned working hours, break schedules, and dismissal time.
  • Inform the supervisor if unable to report on time before the scheduled start of work.

👔 2. Professional Appearance and Grooming

Guideline: Students must observe proper dress code and personal grooming as required by the partner institution.

  • Wear the prescribed uniform, ID badge, or proper business attire at all times.
  • Maintain neat and appropriate hairstyle and personal hygiene.
  • Avoid excessive accessories, heavy makeup, or distracting hairstyles.

🤝 3. Respect and Professional Conduct

Guideline: Students must show respect to supervisors, coworkers, clients, and everyone in the workplace at all times.

  • Use polite and professional language at all times.
  • Address supervisors and staff with respect and follow proper workplace etiquette.
  • Listen attentively to instructions and ask for clarification politely when unsure.

📱 4. Attitude and Use of Gadgets

Guideline: Maintain a positive attitude toward work responsibilities and minimize distractions during working hours.

  • Mobile phones must be kept on silent mode and used only during break times.
  • Be proactive — ask for tasks when idle rather than waiting to be told what to do.
  • Accept constructive criticism positively and use it to improve performance.

🔒 5. Confidentiality and Workplace Property

Guideline: Respect the privacy of information and the property of the workplace.

  • Do not share sensitive information or documents to unauthorized persons.
  • Do not take photos or videos inside the workplace without permission.
  • Handle equipment with care — report any damage or loss immediately.

⚠️ 6. Safety and Emergency Awareness

Guideline: Observe all safety protocols to ensure personal well-being and that of others in the workplace.

  • Familiarize yourself with emergency exits, first aid kits, and fire extinguishers.
  • Follow all safety signs, warnings, and instructions provided.
  • Report any accidents, injuries, or unsafe conditions to your supervisor immediately.
